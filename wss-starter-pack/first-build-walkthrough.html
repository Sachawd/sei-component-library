Your first build, one screen at a time.

Follow the numbered red circles on each screen. Every line is one click or one key press, and every screen ends with one link to the full Designer Guide Reference if you want the detail.

Screen 1 of 12 · Click by Click Walkthrough

Screen 1: Open your task in ClickUp

  1. 1Open Site Change Requests in ClickUp in your web browser.
  2. 2Click your assigned task to open it.
  3. 3Look at the Brand field to see which store is named, such as WorldSoccerShop or Soccer.com.
  4. 4Look at the Route field to see Exists or Adapt.
  5. 5Read the five line Done means list at the top of the task description.

Screen 2: Lock your Figma frame and copy the link

  1. 1Open your campaign design file in Figma.
  2. 2Click the name of your finished frame on the canvas.
  3. 3Right click the frame name.
  4. 4Move your mouse over Copy/Paste as in the menu.
  5. 5Click Copy link to selection.
  6. 6Click inside the Figma Frame field on your ClickUp task.
  7. 7Press Command V on your keyboard to paste the link.

Screen 3 of 12 · Click by Click Walkthrough

Screen 3: Crop and save new photos in Photo Sizer

  1. 1Open Photo Sizer in your browser.
  2. 2Click the Component dropdown at the top left.
  3. 3Click the block you are building.
  4. 4Drag your photo file from Finder onto the centre canvas.
  5. 5Drag the photo inside the frame until the crop looks right.
  6. 6Click the File name box.
  7. 7Type a short lowercase photo name ending in .jpg.
  8. 8Click the green Download 2x JPG button.
Detail in the reference: Step 2: Sizing Photos in Photo Sizer →
Screen 4 of 12 · Click by Click Walkthrough

Screen 4: Put your files into your Box folder

  1. 1Open the WorldSoccerShop Agent Coding folder in Box.
  2. 2Click New at the top right.
  3. 3Click Folder in the menu.
  4. 4Type your page name in the box.
  5. 5Click Create.
  6. 6Open your new page folder.
  7. 7Create three subfolders named images, working images, and Info.
  8. 8Open the images subfolder.
  9. 9Drag your sized photos from Photo Sizer into the images subfolder.
  10. 10Click Share at the top right of your page folder.
  11. 11Click Copy link.
  12. 12Paste that Box folder link onto your ClickUp task.
Detail in the reference: Step 2: Prep Your Page Folder in Box →

Screen 5: Download your starting code from the Marketing Code Library

  1. 1Open the Sports Endeavors Marketing Code Library in your browser.
  2. 2Click your store tab at the top, such as WorldSoccerShop, Soccer.com, or 431 Sports.
  3. 3Click the card for the component you are adapting.
  4. 4Look at the Fastr CMS Heights and Code card on the right to see the three height numbers.
  5. 5Click the Download HTML button to save the starting code file to your Mac.

Screen 6: Paste the Step Zero prompt into your AI

  1. 1Open the Step Zero prompt in the Designer Guide Reference.
  2. 2Click Copy Prompt.
  3. 3Open your AI chat window in Claude, Cursor, or Antigravity.
  4. 4Click inside the message box.
  5. 5Press Command V on your keyboard to paste the Step Zero prompt.
  6. 6Drag your starting HTML file from Finder into the chat box.
  7. 7Paste your Figma frame link, your Box image list, and your final copy into the chat box.
  8. 8Press Return on your keyboard.

Screen 7: Check every photo in your FINAL file in Safari

  1. 1Read the agent's reply in chat to see the exact FINAL file name, the closing tag, and the three measured heights.
  2. 2Open Finder on your Mac.
  3. 3Click Downloads in the left sidebar.
  4. 4Click the .html file that has the word FINAL and the newest date and time in its name.
  5. 5Right click that FINAL file.
  6. 6Move your mouse over Open With in the menu.
  7. 7Click Safari.
  8. 8Scroll from the top of the page to the bottom to check that every photo loads.

Screen 8 of 12 · Click by Click Walkthrough

Screen 8: Open your FINAL file in Visual Studio Code and copy all

  1. 1Close the Safari preview tab.
  2. 2Open Visual Studio Code on your Mac (or download it free from code.visualstudio.com).
  3. 3Click File in the top menu bar.
  4. 4Click Open.
  5. 5Click Downloads in the left sidebar.
  6. 6Click your newest FINAL file.
  7. 7Click Open.
  8. 8Click once anywhere inside the code text on the right.
  9. 9Press Command A on your keyboard to highlight every line from the very top to the very bottom.
  10. 10Scroll to the last line to see the closing tag the agent named in chat.
  11. 11Press Command C on your keyboard to copy all the highlighted code.

Screen 9 of 12 · Click by Click Walkthrough

Screen 9: Paste the code and 3 heights into Fastr CMS

  1. 1Open Fastr CMS in your browser.
  2. 2Click your own test component to open it.
  3. 3Click inside the HTML code box.
  4. 4Press Command A on your keyboard to highlight the old code in the box.
  5. 5Press Delete on your keyboard so the code box is empty.
  6. 6Press Command V on your keyboard to paste your new code into the box.
  7. 7Scroll down to Reserved height.
  8. 8Type the agent's three numbers into the Mobile, 640px and up, and 1024px and up boxes.
  9. 9Click Save.
  10. 10Click the Reserves space tab under Embed code.
  11. 11Click Copy.
  12. 12Paste that embed onto your Fastr test page.
Detail in the reference: Step 3: Build on Your Test Page and Reserved Heights →
Screen 10 of 12 · Click by Click Walkthrough

Screen 10: Check your test page in a second browser and in Responsively

  1. 1Open your Fastr test page link in Safari.
  2. 2Scroll from top to bottom on the page.
  3. 3Open Chrome on your Mac.
  4. 4Paste the same test page link into the Chrome address bar.
  5. 5Press Return on your keyboard.
  6. 6Open Responsively App on your Mac.
  7. 7Paste your Fastr test page link into the top address bar in Responsively.
  8. 8Press Return on your keyboard.
  9. 9Scroll down across all five widths (360px, 390px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px).
  10. 10Click every tab, button, and pop up on the page.

Screen 11 of 12 · Click by Click Walkthrough

Screen 11: Record a Jam of desktop and phone

  1. 1Open your Fastr test page in Chrome.
  2. 2Click the Jam icon in the top right toolbar of Chrome (or install it from jam.dev).
  3. 3Click Record video.
  4. 4Click Start recording.
  5. 5Scroll slowly from the top of the page to the bottom on desktop.
  6. 6Click each tab or pop up on the page once.
  7. 7Switch to the 390px phone view.
  8. 8Scroll from top to bottom again.
  9. 9Click Stop to finish the recording.
  10. 10Click Copy link.

Screen 12 of 12 · Click by Click Walkthrough

Screen 12: Create your Code Share link and hand in on ClickUp

  1. 1Open Code Share in your browser.
  2. 2Click + Paste & share a new file at the top right.
  3. 3Click inside the code box.
  4. 4Press Command V on your keyboard to paste the code from your FINAL file.
  5. 5Click Create share link & copy.
  6. 6Open your task in Site Change Requests in ClickUp.
  7. 7Click the HTML Code field in the Links row.
  8. 8Press Command V on your keyboard to paste your Code Share link.
  9. 9Paste your Fastr test page link, your Jam link, your Box folder link, the requester's yes, and the agent's Pass or Fail check list onto the task.
  10. 10Click the task status button at the top left.
  11. 11Click UX review.
  12. 12Send Anthony a message with your Fastr test page link and the QA Pre-Read.
