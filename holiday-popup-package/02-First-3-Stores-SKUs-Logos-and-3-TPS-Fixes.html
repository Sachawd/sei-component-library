Why the Tampa Super Cup CSS Fix Must Apply to All Holiday Pop-Up, CFCS & Goal Club Stores:
  • On live Soccer.com and WorldSoccerShop pages, legacy stylesheet rules use #body h1..h6, #body p, #body ul li::before, and #body a:visited (specificity 0,1,0,1), which outrank single-class wrappers like .gcd or .bfc (specificity 0,0,1,0).
