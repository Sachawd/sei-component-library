Cabana 8 to V2

Every Cabana 8 component and the V2 piece it becomes, so developers know what changed and what stays.

How to read this map.

Cabana 8 is V1. V2 uses shadcn's own names, as agreed with Neil. Rows marked "Proposed" or "check with Neil" are Components' best match, not decided. Nothing in Cabana 8 is copied into V2. Each part is rebuilt on the V2 colours when its turn comes.

32V2 parts they become. Many Cabana pieces merge into one part.
22V2 blocks: store sections built from those parts.

Three rules for developers

  • Use the V2 name in code. The Cabana name is only for finding the old design.
  • One part, many looks. Cabana's separate components become options (variant, size) on one part.
  • Never hard-code a colour. Every part reads the V2 variables, so the three brands switch on their own.
