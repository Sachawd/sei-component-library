Decisions at the top (Fri 30 Oct 2026)

Ruled by Sacha on Friday 30 October 2026 at 12:40pm and 12:45pm PT.

Ruled · 30 Oct 2026

1. The 42-variable shape is accepted with two conditions

  • Drop the 68-role V2 alias layer and use 42 variables directly: shadcn's 32 variables, radius, a sans font, a heading font, plus Neil's 7 custom colours (brand, loyalty, sale, cart, info, success, warning).
  • Condition A (measured values, not opacity fades): text, borders, and focus rings get real measured values per brand rather than opacity fades (90, 80, 50, and 10 percent). The failing border (1.26 to 1) and brand 10% tint are fixed before agreed becomes done.
  • Condition B (plain job words): custom names keep a plain job word, which Neil's seven do today.
Ruled · 30 Oct 2026

2. Dark mode: out of this web round, door kept open

  • Web dark mode is out of this round, with the door kept open (nothing hard-coded that stops a dark block later).
  • Email dark mode lives inside the email templates, not in V2 (Components checks Brock's holiday templates in dark mode before handoff).
  • Open question in Sacha's reply to Neil: Neil's page says no dark mode was decided on 9 September, which is not written down in the Design System canon.
Ruled · 30 Oct 2026

3. Nutmeg component hub and Figma variables: go

  • Document eight components in Nutmeg on Neil's published shadcn recipes across three brands (Soccer.com, WorldSoccerShop, 431 Sports), then compose one PDP.
  • Figma side started (30 Oct, 1:05pm PT): Sacha's new Figma file (Nutmeg | SE V2 Design System) now carries a V2 tokens variable collection with 3 modes (Soccer.com, WorldSoccerShop, 431 Sports) and 36 placeholder variables wired to Neil's token paths across 440 component bindings and 340 primitive bindings.
  • Weekend web scope: Nutmeg hub shell, Button, Input, Card to the full template, and the estate index of everything built so far.
