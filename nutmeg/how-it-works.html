How It Works

1

Check the brief

Open the task in ClickUp Site Change Requests so you have the approved copy, launch date, and product picks before you start.

2

Design in Figma

Pull a pre-coded block from Cabana 8 into Figma, lay out your content with Auto Layout, and size your JPG photos in Photo Sizer.

3

Adapt the code with AI

Give your AI the starting HTML file from Volley, the Step Zero prompt, and your Link Book URLs so it swaps in your words and photos.

4

Paste into your test page

Check your final HTML file in Safari, copy it from Visual Studio Code into Fastr with the three heights, and place the Reserves space embed on your test page.

5

Test on desktop and phone

Check your test page in a second browser and across five screen widths in Responsively, then record a quick Jam video.

6

Hand in one link on ClickUp

Paste your code into Code Share to create one link for ClickUp and move the task to UX review so your site author can publish it live.
