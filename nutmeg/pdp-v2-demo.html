Every motion on this page, and the token behind it

Two families, and you should be able to feel the seam. Store curves are Material 3 and Carbon verbatim, read 2026-09-05. Story springs are generated and written as linear(). Sandbox, not production - nothing here ships today.

feedback.success Bag count and wishlist heart 388ms · linear() spring, damping 0.62 · peak 1.083

Reduced motion is handled once, at token level - the durations and displacements are redefined inside a single prefers-reduced-motion block and every rule that reads them follows. No component has to remember. Turn it on in your OS and the page goes still, except the focus ring, which shortens rather than disappearing.

Two rules about screen size. Durations never change between breakpoints - 200ms feels like 200ms on any device. Distances do - the 8px card lift drops to 4px under 768px, because 8px of travel reads very differently on a 375px screen. And hover is gated on @media (hover: hover) rather than a width, since plenty of wide tablets have no cursor.
