Your first build

Follow one build from ClickUp to Fastr, one screen and one click at a time.

One click or key press per row.

Every screen below lists the exact actions in order and links to the full rule in the Designer guide (updated 20 Oct 2026).

Screen 1: Open your task in ClickUp

Confirm the brand, route, and five-line Done means list before starting.

ClickWhat you do
1Open Site Change Requests in ClickUp in your web browser.
2Click your assigned task to open it.
3Look at the Brand field to see which store is named, such as WorldSoccerShop or Soccer.com.
4Look at the Route field to confirm it reads Exists or Adapt.
5Read the five-line Done means list at the top of the task description.

Full rules: Step 1: Check the brief.

Screen 2: Lock your Figma frame and copy the link

Copy the direct link to your finished frame in Figma and paste it onto your ClickUp task.

ClickWhat you do
1Open your campaign design file in Figma.
2Click the name of your finished frame on the canvas.
3Right-click the frame name.
4Move your mouse over Copy/Paste as in the menu.
5Click Copy link to selection.
6Click inside the Figma Frame field on your ClickUp task.
7Press Command V on your keyboard to paste the link.

Full rules: Step 2: Design and prepare Box.

Screen 3: Crop and save new photos in Photo Sizer

Size new photos at 2x as JPG files under the weight limit (300 KB max for desktop heroes, under 200 KB for all others).

ClickWhat you do
1Open Photo Sizer in your browser.
2Click the Component dropdown at the top left.
3Click the block you are building.
4Drag your photo file from Finder onto the center canvas.
5Drag the photo inside the frame until the crop looks right.
6Click the File name box.
7Type a short lowercase photo name ending in .jpg.
8Click the Download 2x JPG button.

Full rules: Step 2: Images and speed rules.

Screen 4: Put your files into your Box folder

Create your page folder and three subfolders in Box, upload your sized photos, and link the folder on ClickUp.

ClickWhat you do
1Open the Agent Coding folder in Box.
2Click New at the top right.
3Click Folder in the menu.
4Type your page name in the box.
5Click Create.
6Open your new page folder.
7Create three subfolders named images, working images, and Info.
8Open the images subfolder.
9Drag your sized photos from Photo Sizer into the images subfolder.
10Click Share at the top right of your page folder.
11Click Copy link.
12Paste that Box folder link onto your ClickUp task.

Full rules: Step 2: Design and prepare Box.

Screen 5: Download your starting code

Pick the verified block in the code library, check its three baseline heights, and save the starting HTML file.

ClickWhat you do
1Open All blocks in your browser.
2Click your store tab at the top, such as WorldSoccerShop, Soccer.com, or All brands.
3Click the card for the block you are adapting.
4Look at the Fastr heights section to see the three baseline height numbers.
5Scroll to The code and click Copy code (or download the starting HTML file from the old library view).

Full rules: Before you start: How to get your starting code file.

Screen 6: Paste the Step Zero prompt into your AI

Give your AI the Step Zero rules, your starting HTML file, your Figma frame link, and your Box image list.

ClickWhat you do
1Open the Step Zero AI prompt in the Designer guide.
2Click Copy code on the prompt box.
3Open your AI chat window in Claude, Cursor, or Antigravity.
4Click inside the message box.
5Press Command V on your keyboard to paste the Step Zero prompt.
6Drag your starting HTML file from Finder into the chat box.
7Paste your Figma frame link, your Box image list, and your final copy into the chat box.
8Press Return on your keyboard.

Full rules: Step Zero AI prompt.

Screen 7: Check every photo in your FINAL file in Safari

Open the FINAL file from Downloads in Safari before pasting into Fastr to verify every photo loads (Sacha, 20 Oct 2026).

ClickWhat you do
1Read the agent's reply in chat to see the exact FINAL file name, the closing tag, and the three measured heights.
2Open Finder on your Mac.
3Click Downloads in the left sidebar.
4Click the .html file that has the word FINAL and the newest date and time in its name.
5Right-click that FINAL file.
6Move your mouse over Open With in the menu.
7Click Safari.
8Scroll from the top of the page to the bottom to check that every photo loads cleanly.

Full rules: Step 4: Get the final code.

Screen 8: Open your FINAL file in Visual Studio Code and copy all

Open the FINAL file in a code editor, highlight from first line to last, verify the closing tag, and copy.

ClickWhat you do
1Close the Safari preview tab.
2Open Visual Studio Code on your Mac (or Cursor, Antigravity IDE, or BBEdit).
3Click File in the top menu bar.
4Click Open.
5Click Downloads in the left sidebar.
6Click your newest FINAL file.
7Click Open.
8Click once anywhere inside the code text on the right.
9Press Command A on your keyboard to highlight every line from top to bottom.
10Scroll to the last line to confirm it matches the closing tag the agent named in chat.
11Press Command C on your keyboard to copy all the highlighted code.

Full rules: Step 4: Get the final code.

Screen 9: Paste the code and three heights into Fastr CMS

Clear the HTML box, paste your new code, enter the three heights, save, and copy the Reserves space embed tab.

ClickWhat you do
1Open Fastr CMS in your browser.
2Click your own test component to open it.
3Click inside the HTML code box.
4Press Command A on your keyboard to highlight the old code in the box.
5Press Delete on your keyboard so the code box is empty.
6Press Command V on your keyboard to paste your new code into the box.
7Scroll down to Reserved height.
8Type the agent's three numbers into the Mobile, 640px and up, and 1024px and up boxes.
9Click Save.
10Click the Reserves space tab under Embed code.
11Click Copy.
12Paste that embed onto your Fastr test page.

Full rules: Step 3: Build on your test page.

Screen 10: Check your test page in a second browser and in Responsively

Confirm the save landed in a second browser and check all five screen widths in Responsively App.

ClickWhat you do
1Open your Fastr test page link in Safari.
2Scroll from top to bottom on the page.
3Open Chrome on your Mac.
4Paste the same test page link into the Chrome address bar.
5Press Return on your keyboard.
6Open Responsively App on your Mac.
7Paste your Fastr test page link into the top address bar in Responsively.
8Press Return on your keyboard.
9Scroll down across all five widths (360px, 390px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px).
10Click every tab, button, and pop-up on the page.

Full rules: Step 5: Test and record a Jam.

Screen 11: Record a Jam of desktop and phone

Record one Jam video showing desktop, 390px phone, and every interactive state.

ClickWhat you do
1Open your Fastr test page in Chrome.
2Click the Jam icon in the top right toolbar of Chrome (or install it from jam.dev).
3Click Record video.
4Click Start recording.
5Scroll slowly from the top of the page to the bottom on desktop.
6Click each tab or pop-up on the page once.
7Switch to the 390px phone view.
8Scroll from top to bottom again.
9Click Stop to finish the recording.
10Click Copy link.

Full rules: Step 5: Test and record a Jam.

Screen 12: Create your Code Share link and hand in on ClickUp

Turn your FINAL HTML file into one Code Share link, paste all seven hand-in items on ClickUp, and move the status to UX review.

ClickWhat you do
1Open Code Share in your browser.
2Click + Paste & share a new file at the top right.
3Click inside the code box.
4Press Command V on your keyboard to paste the code from your FINAL file.
5Click Create share link & copy.
6Open your task in Site Change Requests in ClickUp.
7Click the HTML Code field in the Links row.
8Press Command V on your keyboard to paste your Code Share link.
9Paste your Fastr test page link, your Jam link, your Box folder link, the requester's approval, and the agent's Pass or Fail check list onto the task.
10Click the task status button at the top left.
11Click UX review.
12Send Anthony a message with your Fastr test page link and the QA Pre-Read link (he logs results on Jira ticket WEB-16706).

Full rules: Step 6: Hand in seven items.
