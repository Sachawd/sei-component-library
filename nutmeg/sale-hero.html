Sale hero

Two photo stacks slide in from the left and right on a 4.5 second beat, with a live animated headline and button.

Live demo

Switch the screen width. Photos show only once they are on the image host.

Open in a new tab

How it's built

Two photo stacks slide in from the left and right on a 4.5 second beat, with a live animated headline and button. Photos are not on the image host yet, so the preview shows none.

Fastr heights

Type these into the three Reserved height boxes.

560pixels on phones (Mobile box).
680pixels from 640 wide.
808pixels from 1024 wide.
